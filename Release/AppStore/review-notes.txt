Mirivo is a player for user-provided authorized media. No Mirivo account or sign-in is required. No IPTV accounts, channels or content subscription are supplied.

Playback review: Sources > Add source > Stream link. Name: Apple HLS Test. URL: https://devstreaming-cdn.apple.com/videos/streaming/examples/bipbop_adv_example_hevc/master.m3u8 . This is Apple’s public developer test stream from https://developer.apple.com/streaming/examples/ . Open the source, select the stream, and test play/pause, seeking and full-screen playback. M3U playlists and Xtream Codes sources are also supported; provider credentials remain in the device Keychain.

CarPlay: this candidate has the CarPlay Audio entitlement only. Add a source on iPhone, open Mirivo > Sources in CarPlay and use Now Playing audio controls. This candidate does not offer CarPlay video or screen mirroring. Pro does not grant CarPlay Video entitlement or vehicle compatibility.

Pro: Settings > Mirivo Pro. Yearly is a 1-year auto-renewable subscription billed once per year. Lifetime is a non-consumable one-time purchase. Both provide identical Pro features. Free: 2 hours daily viewing, 1 saved source, 10 minutes per screen broadcast. Pro removes these limits and enables vehicle mode and supported on-device captions. Restore Purchases and Apple subscription management are available. Buying Lifetime does not automatically cancel an existing yearly subscription.

Privacy, Terms and Help open the current Netlify pages from Settings and Pro, and require internet access. Captions run on-device, require iOS 26 and supported device/language. Media, source credentials, audio and captions are not uploaded to a developer server. RevenueCat processes anonymous purchase history; Firebase provides update checks, optional notifications and explicitly opted-in usage analytics. No ads or cross-app advertising tracking.

Store screenshots were captured from the actual iPhone UI with an original Mirivo test video and temporary demo playlist. The demo media is not a bundled channel service.
