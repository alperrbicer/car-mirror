Mirivo plays user-provided, authorized media. No Mirivo account or sign-in is required. Mirivo supplies no channels, IPTV accounts or content subscriptions.

Access: adding sources and browsing the library are available before subscribing. Playback, offline downloads and screen sharing require an active App Store free trial or verified Pro access. There is no daily free-viewing or ten-minute broadcast allowance in this candidate.

Products: com.alperbicer.carmirror.pro.weekly (one week, eligible-user trial: 3 days) and com.alperbicer.carmirror.pro.yearly (one year, eligible-user trial: 7 days) provide identical Pro features. They belong to subscription group 22438082 at the same service level. Prices and introductory-offer eligibility are read from StoreKit. An account can redeem only one introductory offer per subscription group. The paywall shows the actual trial, subsequent price, billing period and auto-renewal terms before Apple confirmation. Restore Purchases and Apple subscription management are available. Previous valid lifetime transactions remain supported for restore; the lifetime plan is not offered for new purchases in the candidate.

After obtaining sandbox Pro access or an eligible trial, open the source and test play/pause, seeking and full-screen playback. Do not use a real-money purchase for review. M3U playlists and Xtream Codes are also supported. Offline downloads work only for eligible non-live, unprotected video in supported formats and require the provider's permission.

CarPlay video requires a compatible vehicle, iPhone and system and is available only while parked. A standard CarPlay connection alone does not provide video support. Mirivo does not bypass DRM, vehicle restrictions or source-app capture restrictions. Live captions use on-device models and require iOS 26 and a supported device/language.

Trial reminders: after a verified free-trial transaction, the app may ask for iOS notification permission and schedule an optional local reminder 48 hours before expiry. Notification permission is not required for purchase or access. The reminder can be disabled in Settings. Delivery depends on iOS settings; the customer must manage cancellation in the App Store.

Privacy, Terms and Help open the current Netlify pages from Settings and Pro and require internet access. Source credentials, favorites and continuation records stay in device-only Keychain; library lists may be cached in encrypted files locally. Selected media is sent to the chosen receiver/provider, not to a Mirivo server. RevenueCat handles purchase verification/analytics; Firebase provides update checks, optional notifications and opt-in usage analytics. There are no ads or cross-app advertising tracking.
