Mirivo uses ReplayKit broadcast upload, AVFoundation, public CarPlay APIs and optional real external-display scenes. It does not bypass DRM, modify CarPlay, or supply content. App Group is required for ephemeral session state and local diagnostics. Media stays in bounded memory; the local HTTP endpoint uses a per-session secret path. ATS media exceptions support user-provided streams. Source credentials remain in Keychain.

Mirivo is ad-free and includes optional Pro purchases. Free access: 2 hours of daily viewing, 1 saved source, and 10 minutes per broadcast. Pro removes these limits and unlocks vehicle mode and supported live captions. Weekly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions offer identical Pro features; lifetime is a non-consumable one-time purchase. Open Settings > Mirivo Pro to purchase or restore. Prices are supplied by App Store. Verified StoreKit 2 entitlements control access on the phone and ReplayKit extension. RevenueCat observes transactions only when its Apple SDK key is configured. No Mirivo account or content-provider subscription is included.

No Mirivo account is required. A reviewer can exercise the library using a stream or M3U URL they have permission to access. No bundled channel lists, service credentials or copyrighted test feeds are supplied. Offline Privacy, Terms and Help are in Settings.

Current default build: CarPlay Audio. Apple's account approval was supplied by the owner; the development-signed main app and embedded profile now contain com.apple.developer.carplay-audio without carplay-video. The broadcast extension contains only the App Group. This does not establish App Store distribution signing or vehicle acceptance.

Audio review flow: add a permitted stream or playlist in Sources on iPhone. Open Mirivo > Sources in CarPlay, select a channel and use the system Now Playing controls. Play, pause and stop are connected to the app's player. Video actions are excluded from the Audio CarPlay menu.

Screen sharing belongs to the separate Audio + Video build and must not be advertised as a working CarPlay feature of the Audio build. After Video approval and vehicle validation: connect a compatible parked car, open Mirivo on CarPlay, start the system broadcast from the phone, then switch to a permitted source app. Live captions: Settings > Live captions; prepare an available language on a supported iOS 26+ device. Models run on device.

Before submitting: place weekly and yearly in the same subscription level, complete StoreKit/sandbox verification and RevenueCat production mapping, select the intended Audio or Audio + Video build, match metadata/screenshots to its verified features, verify distribution entitlements, and attach supported-vehicle test results and a recorded reviewer walkthrough. A development-signed Audio build is available locally; physical acceptance, distribution and the reviewer walkthrough remain open.
