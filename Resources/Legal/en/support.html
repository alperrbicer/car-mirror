Help & FAQ

Quick answers, connection steps and direct support.

How do I start screen sharing?

Connect your iPhone to your car’s CarPlay connection. Open Mirivo on the car display, then tap the system broadcast control in the phone’s Mirror tab and start the Mirivo broadcast. Switch to your source app. Stop with the Mirivo stop button or the iOS broadcast control.

Why is the CarPlay icon or video missing?

The vehicle, connection, signed CarPlay entitlements and iOS video support all matter. While parked, try a direct cable first. Test a wireless adapter separately. Starting screen capture alone does not confirm video output in the car. Export a diagnostic report from Settings to share with support.

Why does YouTube or another app show a black screen?

Some apps and protected content do not permit screen capture. Mirivo does not bypass DRM. Try other content you may access; source-app terms and system support apply.

Audio is delayed or plays twice.

In Settings → Audio, With video carries audio in the broadcast; From source app preserves the source app’s own audio route. Restart screen sharing after changing the setting. The suitable option can vary by vehicle and source app.

How do I add IPTV or an M3U source?

Open Sources → +. Select M3U playlist, direct stream or Xtream Codes. Enter a name and the provider’s address; Xtream also requires a username and password. Mirivo does not provide content or accounts. Prefer HTTPS; the system may block unencrypted HTTP playlists.

Where are vehicle mode and captions?

While playing a source, Vehicle mode opens a player with large controls and adapts to landscape when you rotate the phone. Source-provided subtitles are available in the player’s subtitle menu. To add speech captions to a screen broadcast, prepare a supported language in Settings → Live captions. The first download needs internet; speech recognition runs on the device.

How do Pro and ads work?

Every Mirivo version is ad-free. Pro sales are currently disabled and available features are free. When sales open, real prices and terms will appear on the purchase screen. Payment requires your confirmation.

How do I remove my data?

In Settings, Clear logs removes technical diagnostics. Delete all sources removes sources and Keychain credentials. Do this before uninstalling. If you shared a report, ask its recipient separately to delete their copy.

What should I send to support?

Email alperrbicer@gmail.com with the iOS and Mirivo versions, car model, wired or wireless connection and steps that reproduce the issue. You may attach the diagnostic report exported from Settings. Do not send IPTV passwords, stream URLs or personal screen content.
