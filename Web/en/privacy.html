Privacy Policy

Mirivo is provided by Alper Biçer. This policy describes data handling in the iOS app and on this support website.

Updated: October 4, 2026

Screen and app audio

Screen sharing begins with the iOS system confirmation. Video and app audio are processed on your device and transmitted to the selected compatible display. Mirivo does not upload them to a developer server or create an audio or video recording. Media fragments are held in bounded temporary memory and removed when the session ends. Mirivo does not record the microphone. Personal information and notifications visible on the screen may be included in the broadcast. You can stop it at any time using the iOS broadcast control.

Local connection

Screen broadcasts use a hard-to-guess, per-session access address. Local transmission may use HTTP; this is not a promise of end-to-end encryption. The access address is excluded from logs and exported diagnostic reports. Access is revoked when sharing stops.

Sources and credentials

Source names and types are stored in the app’s local storage. Stream URLs, usernames and passwords are stored in iOS Keychain with device-only accessibility. Channel lists are loaded into memory. Mirivo does not copy this information to a developer server or provide iCloud synchronization. Your chosen content server receives your IP address, requested content and any necessary credentials when you connect; its own policies apply. HTTP sources do not encrypt transport. Prefer HTTPS when available.

Live captions

When enabled on a supported device and language, live captions use Apple’s on-device speech models. Initial setup may download a language model from Apple. Mirivo does not send speech or generated text to a server or create a persistent transcript. Captions are composited into the video and temporary text is cleared on stop. Recognition may be inaccurate.

Preferences and diagnostics

Language, quality, audio and caption preferences stay on the device. Technical logs retain up to 10 recent sessions within a total 5 MiB budget. They contain structured states, error codes, timing, media counters and connection types. Screen content, audio, captions, source URLs, tokens, IPTV accounts, passwords and device names are excluded. Reports are not sent automatically; you select the recipient in the system share sheet. Mirivo uses no advertising SDK. When RevenueCat is configured, its purchase SDK processes a random app user identifier, purchase and subscription records, and technical connection information to verify and manage Pro purchases. Source credentials, screen content, audio and captions are not sent to RevenueCat.

Purchases

Apple App Store handles payment and subscriptions. Mirivo does not receive payment card details. Access is determined from locally verified StoreKit transactions.

Support and this website

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send are used to respond. You do not need to send screen recordings, stream passwords or personal content. Messages are retained as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. This static website has no ads, analytics cookies, forms or tracking scripts. When hosted on Netlify, Netlify may process technical connection details, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the website under its own privacy policy.

Deletion and choices

Settings lets you clear diagnostics and delete all sources together with saved credentials. Individual sources can also be removed. Use these controls before uninstalling the app; iOS Keychain entries can survive app removal. Mirivo does not automatically back up local data to a Mirivo account. Contact us below regarding support information you sent or applicable access, correction and deletion rights.

Contact and updates
