Devices and compatibility

The iPhone app targets iOS 18 or later. The official CarPlay video route requires iOS 26.4 and a supporting vehicle and connection. Live captions require iOS 26 and a supported on-device language. Mirivo does not remove DRM or source-app restrictions. In-car video must only be used while parked and when permitted by the system.

Free version and Pro

The free tier includes two hours of daily viewing, one saved source and ten minutes per broadcast. Vehicle mode is also available in the free tier within the same daily viewing allowance. Pro removes these limits and adds supported live captions. Mirivo has no ads. The purchase screen shows available plans, localized prices and renewal terms. Paid access begins only after you confirm an Apple purchase. Pro does not guarantee access on unsupported devices or to protected content.
